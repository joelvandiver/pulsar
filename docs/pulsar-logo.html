PULSAR

Every crab needs a shell.

named for the Crab Pulsar · PSR B0531+21

Why this mark

It's a real star

The Crab Pulsar is the neutron star at the heart of the Crab Nebula — a crab, and a pulsar. The name was doing the design work already.

The claws

Two open pincers in Rust orange hold the star — the Rustacean, and the shell a crab lives in. They double as brackets around the core.

The pulse

A pulsar is a lighthouse: twin beams and arriving wavefronts. The arcs fade with distance — and pulse, if you watch the header.
